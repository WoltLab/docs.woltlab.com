    Migrating from WoltLab Suite 6.2 - Forum#

    WoltLab Suite Forum 6.3 renders the posts of a thread, of a user, of the deleted content and of the moderation queue through list views. The legacy thread page with its clipboard, its inline editor and its jQuery based dialogs has been removed, together with js/WBB.js.

    Posts are changed through commands and their interactions instead of wbb\data\post\PostAction, see Database Object Builder. Editing a post and the quick reply are form builder forms now.

    Post List Views#

    Page Controller List view
    Thread wbb\page\ThreadPage wbb\system\listView\user\ThreadPostListView
    Posts of a user wbb\page\UserPostListPage wbb\system\listView\user\UserPostListView
    Deleted posts in the moderation wcf\page\DeletedContentListViewPage wbb\system\listView\user\DeletedPostListView
    Reported and disabled posts in the moderation queue wcf\form\ModerationReportForm, wcf\form\ModerationActivationForm wbb\system\listView\user\ModerationPostListView

    All of them extend wbb\system\listView\user\AbstractPostListView and render their items with the template postListItems. The list views fire the events ThreadPostListViewInitialized, UserPostListViewInitialized, DeletedPostListViewInitialized and ModerationPostListViewInitialized in wbb\event\listView\user.

    AbstractPostListView offers methods that the list views override to change how the posts are shown, for example isCollapsedDeletedPost(), isCollapsedIgnoredPost(), showDeleteNote(), showThreadTitle(), showAds(), canReplyToPosts() and createInteractionProvider(). The bulk interactions are available if the user has one of the moderator permissions in wbb\data\board\Board::POST_BULK_INTERACTION_PERMISSIONS (Board::canUsePostBulkInteractions()). The number of posts per page is the user's postsPerPage, or the setting of the board for the posts of a thread.

    ThreadPostListView marks the thread as read when it renders its items, which covers every request that loads posts of the thread. It limits guests to the first WBB_THREAD_GUEST_MAX_POSTS posts of a thread, on the page of the thread as well as when new posts are loaded.

    Thread Page#

    wbb\page\ThreadPage extends wcf\page\AbstractListViewPage and creates the ThreadPostListView in createListView(). Its public properties are $threadID, $thread, $board, $post, $quickReplyForm, $tags and $similarThreads. The properties $likeData, $similarThreadList, $modificationLogList, $bestAnswerOnCurrentPage, $officialPostMapping, $itemsPerPage, $sortOrder, $postID and $numberOfHiddenPosts have been removed, as well as the template variables defaultSmilies, hasMarkedItems, likeData, similarThreadList, modificationLogList, threadFormOptions, bestAnswerOnCurrentPage, officialPostMapping, attachmentHandler and tmpHash. The list view provides the data of the posts and the modification log entries between them, ThreadPostListView::getNumberOfHiddenPosts() and ThreadPostListView::getFirstOfficialPostLink() replace $numberOfHiddenPosts and $officialPostMapping.

    wbb\page\UserPostListPage extends AbstractListViewPage as well and creates a UserPostListView for the user in $user.

    Deleted Posts and the Moderation Queue#

    wbb\system\moderation\DeletedPostProvider extends wcf\system\moderation\AbstractDeletedContentListViewProvider and returns a DeletedPostListView from getListView(). It is registered through the event wcf\event\moderation\DeletedContentProvidersCollecting instead of the object type com.woltlab.wbb.post of the definition com.woltlab.wcf.deletedContent.

    The moderation queue renders the post of a report or of an activation through AbstractPostModerationQueueHandler::renderPost(), which uses a ModerationPostListView instead of the template moderationPost.

    Loading the Data of Posts#

    wbb\data\post\Post extends wcf\data\CollectionDatabaseObject and loads its data on demand for all posts of a list at once, through wbb\data\post\PostCollection:

    • Post::getThread() returns the thread of the post.
    • Post::getAttachments() returns the attachments that are not embedded in the message. It no longer returns a GroupedAttachmentList and no longer takes the parameter $ignoreCache.
    • Post::loadEmbeddedObjects() loads the embedded objects of the posts before their message is rendered.
    • Post::getLogEntry() returns the modification log entry of the deletion of a post in the trash.
    • Post::getPoll() returns the legacy poll of a post, it is deprecated together with legacy polls.
    • Post::getUserProfile() and the reactions of the post are loaded the same way.

    wbb\data\post\ViewablePostList no longer decorates its posts with ViewablePost, it returns Post objects. ViewablePostList, SimplifiedViewablePostList and ViewablePost are deprecated, use wbb\data\post\PostList and Post instead. The methods that enabled the loading of attachments, embedded objects, polls, threads and modification logs no longer have any effect, see Deprecations and Removals.

    wbb\data\post\ThreadPostList and wbb\data\post\DeletedPostList extend PostList instead of ViewablePostList. The constructor of ThreadPostList is unchanged, its posts are sorted by time and postID. wbb\data\post\SearchResultPostList extends PostList and wbb\data\post\SearchResultPost decorates Post. wbb\data\thread\Thread::getBestAnswerPost() returns a Post.

    Post Commands and Events#

    The Forum no longer runs wbb\data\post\PostAction when posts are changed through the interactions of a post list view or by the thread commands, event listeners on its actions are therefore not called for them. Listen to the events fired by the commands instead:

    Operation Command Event
    Publishing posts wbb\command\post\PublishPosts wbb\event\post\PostsPublished
    Enabling posts wbb\command\post\EnablePosts wbb\event\post\PostsEnabled
    Disabling posts wbb\command\post\DisablePosts wbb\event\post\PostsDisabled
    Moving posts to the trash wbb\command\post\SoftDeletePosts wbb\event\post\PostsSoftDeleted
    Restoring posts wbb\command\post\RestorePosts wbb\event\post\PostsRestored
    Deleting posts wbb\command\post\DeletePosts wbb\event\post\PostsDeleted
    Closing a post wbb\command\post\ClosePost wbb\event\post\PostClosed
    Opening a post wbb\command\post\OpenPost wbb\event\post\PostOpened
    Merging posts wbb\command\post\MergePosts wbb\event\post\PostsMerged
    Moving posts to a thread wbb\command\post\MovePosts wbb\event\post\PostsMoved
    Moving posts to a new thread wbb\command\post\MovePostsToNewThread wbb\event\post\PostsMoved
    Copying posts to a thread wbb\command\post\CopyPosts wbb\event\post\PostsCopied
    Marking a post as the best answer wbb\command\post\MarkPostAsBestAnswer wbb\event\post\BestAnswerMarked
    Unmarking the best answer wbb\command\post\UnmarkPostAsBestAnswer wbb\event\post\BestAnswerUnmarked

    The commands that change the state of posts process several posts at once and skip posts that already have the requested state, their events only contain the posts that have been changed. They update the threads of the posts, for example a thread is moved to the trash once all of its posts are in the trash, unless $updateThreads is false, and they write the modification log unless $createModificationLogs is false. EnablePosts sets the time of the posts to the current time if $updateTime is true.

    PostsMoved contains the posts with the id of their previous thread, PostsMerged contains the post that the other posts have been merged into and the merged posts, which no longer exist. CopyPosts neither creates the target thread nor rebuilds it: wbb\command\post\CreateThreadForPostCopy creates an empty thread for the copies, and the thread has to be rebuilt once all posts have been copied. The copies inherit the deleted and disabled state of the target thread.

    The thread commands DeleteThread, DisableThread, EnableThread, RestoreThread and SoftDeleteThread use these commands for the posts of the thread and accept $includePosts = false to only change the thread. EnableThread also accepts $updateTime to set the time of the thread and its posts to the current time.

    The deprecated methods enable(), disable(), trash(), restore(), delete(), deleteCompletely(), close() and open() of PostAction remain available, use the commands instead.

    RPC Endpoints#

    Endpoint Description
    POST /forum/posts/{id}/close Closes the post through ClosePost.
    POST /forum/posts/{id}/open Opens the post through OpenPost.
    POST /forum/posts/{id}/enable Enables the post through EnablePosts.
    POST /forum/posts/{id}/disable Disables the post through DisablePosts.
    POST /forum/posts/{id}/soft-delete Moves the post to the trash through SoftDeletePosts, with an optional reason.
    POST /forum/posts/{id}/restore Restores the post through RestorePosts.
    DELETE /forum/posts/{id} Deletes a post in the trash through DeletePosts.
    POST /forum/posts/{id}/mark-as-best-answer Marks the post as the best answer of its thread.
    POST /forum/posts/{id}/unmark-as-best-answer Unmarks the post as the best answer of its thread.
    POST /forum/posts/copy Copies the posts in postIDs to the thread threadID in chunks of 20 posts, the next request passes the lastPostID of the previous response until it reports completed.
    GET /forum/posts/{id}/ip-log Returns the IP addresses of the author of the post.
    GET /forum/threads/{id}/new-posts Returns the rendered posts of the page pageNo of the thread, which is used to load new posts.

    Interactions#

    The interactions of a post are provided by wbb\system\interaction\user\PostInteractions, the interactions of a post in a thread by wbb\system\interaction\user\ThreadPostInteractions, which adds the quote and the best answer interactions. Plugins add their interactions in a listener of wbb\event\interaction\user\PostInteractionCollecting, which is fired for both providers, or in a subclass that overrides PostInteractions::addContextInteractions().

    The bulk interactions of posts are provided by wbb\system\interaction\bulk\user\PostBulkInteractions and are extended through wbb\event\interaction\bulk\user\PostBulkInteractionCollecting. They replace the clipboard of posts, see Removals.

    The interaction menu in the header of the thread page uses wbb\system\interaction\user\ThreadPageInteractions, which extends ThreadInteractions and fires wbb\event\interaction\user\ThreadInteractionCollecting. ThreadInteractions is no longer final.

    The dialogs of the interactions are form builder dialogs that are provided by actions:

    Interaction Action
    Editing a post wbb\action\PostEditAction
    Enabling a post wbb\action\PostEnableDialogAction
    Enabling a thread wbb\action\ThreadEnableDialogAction
    Merging posts wbb\action\PostMergeDialogAction
    Moving posts to a new thread wbb\action\PostMoveToNewThreadDialogAction
    Moving posts to a thread wbb\action\PostMoveToExistingThreadDialogAction
    Copying posts to a new thread wbb\action\PostCopyToNewThreadDialogAction
    Copying posts to a thread wbb\action\PostCopyToExistingThreadDialogAction

    Post provides the checks canClose(), canMerge(), canMove() and canCopy() for these interactions.

    Editing Posts and the Quick Reply#

    Posts are edited inline through the form wbb\system\form\builder\PostEditForm, which wbb\action\PostEditAction provides and saves through wbb\command\post\UpdatePost. The quick reply is the form wbb\system\form\builder\ThreadQuickReplyForm, which is shown on the thread page and saved by wbb\action\ThreadQuickReplyAction through wbb\command\post\CreatePost. Neither of them runs PostAction any more, the methods beginEdit(), save() and quickReply() of PostAction have been removed.

    The events wbb\event\post\PostEditFormBuilding and wbb\event\post\QuickReplyFormBuilding are fired before the forms are built. Their listeners add form nodes, either to the form itself or to the settings of the message container with the id message. The values are written into the PostBuilder by the save value callbacks of the added fields: values of custom columns of wbb1_post with setCustomProperty(), any other values with setExtensionData(), which a listener of wbb\event\post\PostUpdated or wbb\event\post\PostCreated processes.

    <?php

namespace example\system\event\listener;

use wbb\data\post\PostBuilder;
use wbb\event\post\PostEditFormBuilding;
use wcf\system\form\builder\container\wysiwyg\WysiwygFormContainer;
use wcf\system\form\builder\field\BooleanFormField;

final class PostEditFormBuildingListener
{
    public function __invoke(PostEditFormBuilding $event): void
    {
        $container = $event->form->getNodeById('message');
        \assert($container instanceof WysiwygFormContainer);

        $container->addSettingsNode(
            BooleanFormField::create('examplePinned')
                ->label('example.post.pinned')
                ->value($event->post->examplePinned)
                ->saveValueCallback(static function (PostBuilder $builder, BooleanFormField $field) {
                    $builder->setCustomProperty('examplePinned', $field->getSaveValue());
                })
        );
    }
}

    The ids of added form nodes must be unique, prefix them with the identifier of the package.

    A reply that is written by the author of the last post of the thread shortly after it is merged into that post. The event wbb\event\post\QuickReplyMergeChecking is fired before a reply is merged, interrupting it with preventDefault() creates a new post instead. It replaces the event quickReplyShouldMerge of PostAction.

    Removals#

    The removed classes, methods, properties, JavaScript and templates are listed in Deprecations and Removals.

    PHP#

    wbb\data\post\PostAction no longer implements IClipboardAction, IAttachmentMessageQuickReplyAction, IMessageInlineEditorAction and IMessageQuickReplyParametersAction, wbb\data\thread\ThreadAction no longer implements ISearchAction.

    The clipboard of posts has been removed, together with the object type com.woltlab.wbb.post of the definition com.woltlab.wcf.clipboardItem and its clipboard actions. Use the bulk interactions of the post list views instead.

    JavaScript#

    js/WBB.js has been removed together with the WBB namespace and all of its classes. The modules of the legacy thread page in WoltLabSuite/Forum/Controller, WoltLabSuite/Forum/Handler/Thread, WoltLabSuite/Forum/Ui/Post and WoltLabSuite/Forum/Ui/Thread have been removed as well. The thread page uses the modules in WoltLabSuite/Forum/Component/Post and WoltLabSuite/Forum/Component/Thread instead.

    Templates#

    The templates thread and userPostList have been rewritten, they only contain the container of their list view. The posts are rendered by the template postListItems with the markup of the message list view of Core. Each post is an article.listView__item.messageListView__item with the id post{postID} and its id in data-object-id, its message is in .messageListView__item__message.

    Previous markup Replacement
    article.wbbPost.message.jsMessage article.listView__item.messageListView__item
    .messageBody, .messageText .messageListView__item__message
    data-post-id data-object-id
    data-can-*, data-is-* The interactions of the post
    .wbbPostBestAnswer .wbbPostListView__item--bestAnswer
    .wbbPostOfficial .wbbPostListView__item--official
    .messageGroupStarter .messageListView__item--contentAuthor
    .wbbPostDeleted, .messageCollapsed, .ignoredUserMessage .messageListView__item--collapsed
    .messageDeleted, .messageDisabled {badge type='deleted'} and {badge type='disabled'}
    #messageQuickReply #wbbThreadQuickReplyContainer

    The template events of the posts have been removed together with the templates threadPostList, deletedPostList and moderationPost, and from the template userPostList. postListItems provides these template events instead:

    Event Location
    header In the header of the post, before its toolbar
    badges After the badges of the post
    noticesBeforeMessage After the notices before the message
    beforeMessage Before the message
    noticesAfterMessage After the notices after the message
    afterMessage After the message, before the footer
    footerButtons After the buttons in the footer, before the interaction menu

    The sidebar of a post is the template messageListViewSidebar, see Message List View Sidebar. The template events messageHeaderMetaData, messageStatus, messageQuickOptions, messageHeader, beforeMessageText, afterMessageText, messageFooter, messageFooterNotes, messageFooterButtons, messageFooterButtonsExtra, afterMessageFooterGroup, beforePosts, beforePost and afterPost have no direct replacement. The template event javascriptInclude of thread has been removed.

    The templates threadListItems and threadContentHeaderTitle show the state of a thread through the {badge} template plugin, the classes .wbbThreadListView__item__badge and their styles have been removed.

    The quick reply and the inline editor no longer use the templates threadQuickReply, postInlineEditor, __threadFormInline, __threadFormInlineTab and __messageFormSettingsInlinePost, which have been removed together with their template listeners and the template event beforeWysiwyg. Use the events QuickReplyFormBuilding and PostEditFormBuilding instead, see Editing Posts and the Quick Reply.
